A little about us

Technology, made less intimidating.

Good troubleshooting starts with clear information and a calm first step.

Why TechFix Hub?

TechFix Hub is a learning project created in a BSIT Web Development context. It brings common hardware, software, network, and security troubleshooting topics into one approachable place. The goal is to make technical information easier to navigate while demonstrating a responsive, multi-page web application built with HTML, CSS, and JavaScript.

How to use the hub

  1. Search or browse the guide library by category and difficulty.
  2. Review symptoms and causes to see whether a guide matches your issue.
  3. Follow the steps in order, checking whether each one resolves the problem.
  4. Bookmark helpful guides for quick access and try the IT quiz to test your knowledge.

IT disclaimer

These guides are general educational information, not a diagnosis or substitute for professional technical support. Troubleshooting and repairs can risk data loss, device damage, or personal injury. Back up important data when possible, follow manufacturer instructions, and do not open powered equipment or handle internal components unless you are qualified and it is safe to do so.

If a step feels unsafe or you are unsure, stop and contact a certified computer technician. For suspected account compromise or data theft, use a trusted device and seek appropriate professional support promptly.
